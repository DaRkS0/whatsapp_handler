# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2025-01-01

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you communicate with us via WhatsApp.

---

## Information We Collect

When you message us on WhatsApp, we may collect:

- Your phone number
- Message content you send to us
- Message timestamps
- Basic WhatsApp profile information (such as name, if available)

---

## How We Use Information

We use the collected information only to:

- Respond to your messages
- Provide customer support
- Improve our services
- Communicate important updates related to our service

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## Data Storage and Security

- Messages are processed securely using the WhatsApp Cloud API provided by Meta.
- We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

---

## Data Sharing

Your data is not shared with third parties except:

- When required by law
- When processed through WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.) as part of message delivery

---

## Data Retention

We retain messages only for as long as necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations.

---

## User Rights

You may request:

- Access to your data
- Deletion of your data

To do so, contact us using the same WhatsApp number.

---

## Third-Party Services

This service uses the WhatsApp Cloud API, operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.  
Meta’s privacy policy applies to WhatsApp infrastructure.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.  
Any changes will be posted on this page.

---

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us via WhatsApp.
